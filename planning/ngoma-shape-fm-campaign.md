## P1 - what the firmware actually does with Shape (BLOCK engine)

Read from the binary (FUN_2400b1d8 = BLOCK channel 0; caller FUN_24011950, call
site 0x2401530e-0x24015332; decomp corpus + raw objdump cross-checks). The
additive-vs-generative question is now settled, but NOT the way the FM hypothesis
guessed.

### The voice, per sample (channel 0)

Three phase accumulators, all reset on trigger, all reading the same two tables
(32768-entry no-interp "sine", 512-entry lerp "tri"; roles inferred):

- osc C (state+0x1bc): freq fc (exp pitch law, incl. block-rate sweep env), plus
  common-mode noise FM (grit).
- osc B (state+0x1e0): freq (1 + 2*Shape) * fc, same common-mode noise FM.
  THIS is Shape's frequency action: a pure ratio/detune on a second oscillator.
- osc A (state+0x1b8): freq 5 * f(pitch), heavily noise-FM'd; only its raw
  triangle is used (ring term below).

Per-oscillator FOLD: `fold(x, center) = (|w*(sine + blend*(tri-sine))*0.25 + 0.25
- center| - 0.25) * 4.0` where w = fold drive (Character), blend = table morph
(grit-zoned). Fold centers DIFFER:
- fold_C center = osc C's OWN tri-table value (self-referential, static timbre).
- fold_B center = p4*0.5 + 0.5 where p4 = the PITCH-ENVELOPE output (verified in
  the caller: s5 is loaded from state+0x708, written by the 4-state envelope
  machine at 0x93c/0x940/0x944). So osc B's fold asymmetry SWEEPS during every
  hit - the sideband family blooms and settles over the pitch-envelope time.

Output: `env^3 * (fold_C + (fold_B * (p4*0.5+0.5) * 1.25 - fold_C) * wS)
+ gritZone * (p4 + p4^3) * tri_A * k`, with
`wS = clamp(Shape_sm * 100, 0, 0.4)` (verified in raw asm; decompiler missed the
clamp). The crossfade weight SATURATES almost immediately: above a tiny bottom
zone, wS = 0.4 constant, and Shape's audible action across the throw is ~purely
the RATIO. That saturation zone is the measured dead-zone in the r law
(r = 0.0189*(ccShape - 9.2): below cc~9.2 osc B is inaudible).

### Where the measured lattice comes from (the decisive reindex)

The voice itself mixes the two folded oscillators LINEARLY - no in-voice cross
nonlinearity (p4 is an envelope, not an audio signal; confirmed against the
caller's register loads, killing the "fold center is the other oscillator"
candidate). But every one of the corpus's 14 lattice modes f = fc*(h + k*r)
reindexes EXACTLY as an integer intermod of the two oscillators:

    f(h,k) = (h-k)*fc + k*fB,   fB = fc*(1+r)     [m = h-k, n = k]

e.g. (1,-1) = 2*fc - fB, (1,2) = 2*fB - fc, (3,2) = fc + 2*fB, (3,-1) = 4*fc - fB,
(3,3) = 3*fB, (5,1) = 4*fc + fB. All 14, including the two "fold-only" modes.
So the lattice is the two-oscillator INTERMOD PRODUCT SET, generated by a
nonlinearity DOWNSTREAM of the voice sum (the always-driven output limiter is the
known candidate; corpus was captured at clipper CC48 = limiting). The lattice is
NOT painted additively by the hardware and it is NOT phase-modulation FM either:
it is two coherent parents + products. FM-hypothesis verdict: REFUTED as literal
PM, but the audible intuition was right - the sidebands are generative, coherent,
and evolving, which an additive bank with static fitted magnitudes and arbitrary
equal start phases does not reproduce.

### Why ours reads as muddy (gap list)

1. Our pre-clip signal is 14 quasi-independent partials; through the drive+limiter
   they intermodulate 14x14 (products of products = spray). Hardware's pre-clip
   signal is TWO coherent oscillators (sparse product set). Worst at high clipper.
2. Our sideband amplitudes are static per trigger (kAmpFit, fitted at CC48
   post-limiter). Hardware cross-product amplitudes are DRIVE-DEPENDENT (they are
   made by the limiter) and p4-swept (fold-center bloom) - they evolve within the
   hit beyond the fitted fast-decay classes, and they should be much quieter when
   the output stage is transparent (our clipper=0 default!). We currently play
   CC48-loud sidebands at clean settings: static AND too loud = muddy.
3. Start phases: hardware products have deterministic phase structure
   ((h-k)*phiC + k*phiB + psi(h,k)); ours all start equal (+0.25) = AM-like
   alignment.

### Measured (fresh grit=0 Shape grid, 180 captures, 2026-07-22)

Campaign: ~/repos/trinity-midi-harness/capture_shape_block.py -> data/
shape_block.jsonl + captures/shape_block/. shape {0..127 x11} x note {48,60,72}
x clipper {CC8 transparent, CC48 corpus} x character {48,100}, grit 0, sweep 0,
every CC pinned; 3 reps at shape {40,85}.

INSTRUMENT TRAP (validate-the-instrument, again): the rig's absolute tuning had
drifted ~+0.75 st vs the corpus nominal (fc ~254 Hz at note 60 with CC20=34
pinned, formula says 243.2). Nominal-fc Goertzel reads were off-lobe and
understated every hardware mode; all numbers below use per-capture EMPIRICAL
fc (carrier peak) and r (osc-B peak). The corpus-era analysis pipeline shares
the nominal-fc assumption - open item below.

- Cross modes ((h-k) != 0, k != 0) at CC8 vs CC48: median 0.005 (-46.7 dB,
  n=200). They are ABSENT from the voice; the lattice is made by the clipper.
- Osc-B family (h-k=0): +1.4 dB CC8 vs CC48 (iqr 1.14-1.22) - voice content.
  Carrier harmonics (k=0): +2.5 dB - voice content (rel rises as the carrier
  decompresses).
- Painted-model audit at the corpus point: the kAmpFit cross amplitudes measure
  +13 dB hot vs hardware per-mode (123 pairs). The band-energy grid (84%) was
  blind to this - band energy is carrier-dominated. This, plus arbitrary painted
  phases and full painted level at every clipper setting, is the measured root
  of "muddy waveshaping".
- Within-hit bloom: osc-B family fades IN as 1 - 0.61*exp(-t/20ms) (fit across
  72 cells; predicted 0.63/0.78/0.92/0.97 vs measured 0.63/0.78/0.91/0.97 at
  10/20/40/60 ms) - the p4 fold-center sweep signature. harmC flat (+-6%);
  cross +15% early excess (inside noise, absorbed by their decay classes).
- Determinism: repeat corr 0.97-0.996 at grit 0 - the voice is deterministic;
  per-mode phase spreads 0.3-1.0 rad across reps make a fitted start-phase
  table noise-limited for now.

## P2 - what shipped (2.8.3.77)

The 16-lane SoA NEON kernel and all non-Shape fitted laws stay. Three changes,
each measurement-forced:

1. CROSS LATTICE GOES GENERATIVE (kCrossPaint = 0). The 8 painted cross-mode
   lanes are silenced; the model's own drive + sqrt-limiter stage generates the
   product lattice from the two real partial families. Probed alternatives at
   the corpus point (per-mode, vs hardware): painted +13.0 dB, trimmed-painted
   (0.218) +8.4 dB, generated-only +7.6 dB (iqr +0.7..+15.6) - generated wins
   AND carries real product phases, real within-hit evolution, and
   threshold-tracking across the whole throw for free.
2. CLIPPER STAGE MORPHS ccEff = 8 + 40*clipper (was pinned CC48 + variable
   drive). P4's pinned threshold left the stage non-transparent at clipper=0
   (drive 1 still clipped peaks at th~0.9, spraying products +42 dB above
   hardware's clean output). Bit-identical CC48 tables at clipper=1; truly
   transparent (th 9e9) at 0, matching the hardware knob's own semantics.
   Equal-loudness makeup refit for the morphing stage: makeup = 3.329*(1
   - 2.632211c + 6.107731c^2 - 3.183871c^3)/driveLinear, NET stage unity at
   the clean home, loudness flat to +-0.35 dB, ripple 0.5 dB (mid-throw
   threshold knee). Net gain at clipper=1: 0.3583 (-8.9 dB vs the pre-makeup
   corpus stage; divide out for absolute hardware comparisons).
3. ONSET BLOOM on the osc-B lanes: amp factor 1 - 0.61*exp(-t/(2*sweepTime)),
   one scalar carrier + per-lane baked mask, vmls+vmul per quad. NEON-clean
   (0 suspect hints).
